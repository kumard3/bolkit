# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Bolkit v2 (voice-led)

## Objective
A second, visually and sonically distinct launch video for Bolkit 1.3.1 for Mac.

## Output
- Compositions: composition/ (music only, captions still on) and composition-voice/ (Kumar's cloned voice), generated by build.mjs (VOICE=1 for voiced)
- Renders: brag.mp4 and brag-voice.mp4, 1920x1080, 25.0s

## Source Material
- Project: ~/codes/personal-project/localvoice
- Real UI: meeting transcript and Models renders from the app's own render command with demo data; Listening pill and notch recorder recreated from HUD.swift and NotchHUD.swift
- Verified facts: Mac 1.3.1 released and notarized; iOS submission still WAITING_FOR_REVIEW so "iphone coming soon"; free, no account

## Creative Direction
- Voice-led kinetic captions, one word at a time, synced to measured speech energy (words.json)
- UI as 3D planes with slow drift; blur dissolves on bar lines; breakdown with a single centered phrase
- Avoid the v1 and AgentCursor language: no chrome labels, no progress bar, no left caption plus right screenshot

## Audio
- Original track music/bolkit-lofi-84.wav (84 BPM), bar-locked scene timing
- Voice lines on their own tracks, music ducked to 0.3 of its level under each line
- Sparse SFX, subtle RMS on the background wave

## Hyperframes Instructions
Same contract as before: root data-composition-id, overlapping clip scenes on separate tracks for dissolves, paused GSAP timeline on window.__timelines["main"], audio tags with volume automation. The hyperframes check must pass before rendering; render with --low-memory-mode.
