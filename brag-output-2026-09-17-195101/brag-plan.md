# Brag Plan: Bolkit v2 (voice-led)

## What is this app?
Bolkit is a free Mac app that types what you say (Hindi and English mixed too) and records calls with who said what, with the models running on the Mac.

## The angle
Kumar talks, the video types. Every caption word appears the moment it is spoken, which is the product's own promise shown as the edit itself. The UI floats in 3D behind the words instead of sitting in a grid, and the music is an original late-night lo-fi track with a tanpura drone instead of an upbeat business beat. Deliberately different from the AgentCursor and v1 layout: no top chrome, no progress bar, no left caption with right screenshot.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black frame, the Bolkit wave drawing as a giant faint line, and "so i made my mac type whatever i say." arriving word by word, huge.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A message window in 3D perspective: the real Listening pill, then "Inserted" and a Hinglish line lands.
- The notch recorder with you and others bars, then the real meeting transcript swings in and Priya and Arjun light up on the beat.
- The real Models page tilted in 3D with apple speech, parakeet and gemma on mlx floating off it.
- Breakdown: the music drops, the stage clears, "no account." alone in the middle.

## Outro / punchline
The mark draws, "Bolkit", "it's called bolkit, and it's free.", then mac app out now, github.com/kumard3/scribe, iphone coming soon.

## User flow worth showing
Speak, text lands in any app, record a call, get a named transcript.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (mapped), freeform direction: late-night voice note that types itself
- Interpretation: slow 3D drift and blur dissolves on bar lines, one voice leading, restraint in color

## Duration: 25.0s

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed that the voice sits on
- Music: original track music/bolkit-lofi-84.wav (84 BPM, D Kafi flavour: tanpura drone, electric piano, swung drums, sub bass, bansuri-style phrase), rendered by music/make_music.py, no third-party license
- Treatment: fade in, drums enter at bar 2 (2.857s), breakdown at bar 7 (17.143s), bansuri phrase at bar 8 (20.0s), fade out over the last 1.2s; ducked to 0.3 under each voice line in the voiced cut
- Cue guidance: scene dissolves on bars 1, 3, 5, 7; name highlights on bar 5 beats 1 and 2; chips on bar 6 beats 2 to 4
- Audio-reactive: subtle, music RMS drives the background wave line opacity
- SFX: sparse (drop on insert, two soft clicks on names, one soft impact on the outro)
